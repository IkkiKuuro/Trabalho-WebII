## Brand & Style

The design system projects athletic vitality, relaxed coastal warmth, and contemporary sport-club professionalism. Built for modern sand-court sports hubs (Beach Tennis, Footvolley, Beach Volleyball), it unites high-energy outdoor performance with effortless leisure lifestyle.

The aesthetic fuses modern sports minimalism with tactile, sun-drenched coastal tones. Visuals rely on generous spacing, sweeping warm highlights, crisp ocean-depth contrast, and softly sculpted corners (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`). Interface elements reject heavy industrial grit in favor of clean, sunlit surfaces that feel aerodynamic, welcoming, and intuitive across courtside booking flows, membership passes, and dynamic schedule discovery.

## Colors

The palette captures a tropical afternoon at the courts: intense solar heat balanced by cooling ocean depth and tactile sand neutrals.

- **Primary (`#F97316` / `#EA580C`):** Radiant Terracotta Sunset. Used for primary CTAs, active court filters, highlighted time slots, and high-impact key badges.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` / `#0369A1`):** Ocean Blue. Delivers athletic focus, structural chrome, key contrast elements, and links.
- **Tertiary (`#FBBF24`):** Solar Sand Gold. Accents ratings, daytime badges, court lighting status, and secondary micro-highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDFBF7` & `#F5EFEB`):** Fine dune sand. Replaces stark corporate white with sun-washed organic warmth for backgrounds, card containers, and soft dividers.
- **Deep Contrast Base (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`):** Deep marine slate for maximum typography legibility, borders on interaction, and structural grounding.

## Typography

The type scale relies exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, harnessing its geometric clarity, open apertures, and modern athletic rhythm.

- **Headlines:** Set in Bold (700) and ExtraBold (800) with slight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to inject urgency and competitive dynamism into match headers, court availability, and tournament showcases.
- **Body:** Uses regular (400) and medium (500) weights with generous line height for effortless readability under varied outdoor sunlight and mobile contexts.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Set with deliberate positive letter-spacing (`0.02em` - `0.06em`) in semi-bold and bold, ideal for badges (e.g., "BEACH TENNIS", "QUADRA 03", "DISPONÍVEL").

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column fluid grid system governs wide viewports, collapsing gracefully into 8 columns on tablet devices (640px to 1024px) and 4 columns on mobile handsets (&lt;640px).

- **Page Layout:** Outer gutters stay tight on mobile (`1rem`) to maximize screen area for booking schedules and modality cards, expanding to `2rem` on desktop with a max-width container of `1280px`.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an intentional 4px/8px modular base. Dense information clusters (scoreboards, court slot pickers) rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while modality presentation showcases and registration steps leverage `space-lg` and `space-xl` to preserve an airy, resort-grade sports feel.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy emphasizes clean, sun-permeated surfaces rather than heavy, artificial shadow drops.

- **Tonal Layers:** The background sits on bleached sand (`#FDFBF7`). Elevated surface cards utilize pristine pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or warmed sand cardstock (`#F5EFEB`).
- **Warm Ambient Diffusion:** Shadows are subtle and tinted with warm earth tones instead of dead grays:
  - *Default Card Elevation:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.04)`
  - *Floating CTAs & Modals:* `0 20px 35px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -2px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.08)`
- **Hairline Sand Borders:** Surfaces feature crisp, ultra-subtle border strokes (`1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` or `1px solid rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.15)` when focused/active) to maintain structure in high-glare environments.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic, energetic geometry with standard roundedness set to Level 2:
- Standard elements (inputs, select triggers, buttons) utilize **0.75rem to 1rem** corners (`rounded-xl`).
- High-level containers, modality cards, and registration panels scale to **1.25rem to 1.5rem** (`rounded-2xl`).
- Tags, sport indicator chips, and circular icon triggers utilize continuous full rounds (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#F97316` transitioning to `#EA580C` on hover. Pure white text, font weight 700, padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`, `rounded-xl`. Enhanced with subtle warm glow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 10px 20px -5px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined with 1.5px `#0284C7` border, `#0284C7` text, transparent background. Fills to light ocean tint (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08)`) on hover.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No border, `#0F172A` text, shifts to `#F5EFEB` on hover.

### Modality Cards (Beach Tennis, Futevôlei, Vôlei de Praia)
- **Structure:** Rounded container (`rounded-2xl`), white base, `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Visuals:** Top cover area features immersive imagery or sport iconography paired with an active pill tag (e.g., "4 Quadras Cobertas", "Aulas & Day-use").
- **State Changes:** Hover induces a `-4px` vertical lift accompanied by a warm golden-sand ring accent (`outline: 2px solid #F97316`).

### Form Inputs & Interactive Registration
- **Input Fields:** Built with `#FFFFFF` background, `0.875rem` vertical padding, `1.25rem` horizontal padding, `rounded-xl`.
- **Resting Border:** `1.5px solid #E2D9CE`.
- **Focus State:** `border-color: #F97316`, accompanied by an outer halo `box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.15)`.
- **Labels:** Set in `label-lg`, positioned outside the input container in `#0F172A` with mandatory asterisks tinted `#EA580C`.

### Chips & Sport Filters
- **Filter Pills:** Compact (`rounded-full`), `padding: 0.5rem 1.25rem`, typography in `label-md`.
- **Inactive:** Sand tint `#F5EFEB`, text `#64748B`.
- **Active:** Background `#0F172A` with `#FFFFFF` text, or Sunset Terracotta `#F97316` with `#FFFFFF` text to signify current modality context.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Radio Court / Level Selector:** Custom card selector; when checked, shifts background to `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.06)`, border becomes `2px solid #F97316`, and reveals an orange indicator mark.
- **Checkboxes:** 20px by 20px, `rounded-md`, filled with `#F97316` when active, with high-contrast white checkmark icon.

### Court Availability Slot Badges
- **Available:** Light emerald-sand background with deep forest green text.
- **Fast-Filling / Sunset Prime:** Soft yellow-gold background (`#FEF3C7`), text `#B45309`, labeled "Horário Nobre".
- **Reserved:** Low opacity `#E2E8F0`, crossed line with slate muted text.
